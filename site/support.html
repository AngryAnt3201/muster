Questions

How do I play?

Each round, drag unit cards from the shop at the bottom onto your half of the field. Drag a placed unit to move it, or tap it to rank it up or sell it. When you are ready, tap Fight and the battle plays out on its own. Units come back every round, so your army grows. The loser of each round takes damage; the first commander to reach zero loses the match.

What are ranks and Ascended units?

A placed unit can be ranked up from Recruit to Veteran to Elite, getting tougher and stronger each time. From round 5 you can Ascend it to rank 4, which unlocks that unit’s signature ability.

Which modes are there?

Campaign (48 levels in eight acts, with a boss at the end of each act), the Daily Challenge (one board a day, the same for every player, with a streak to keep), Gauntlet (rival after rival on one health pool, with boons between fights), Breakpoint (one-battle puzzles with fixed gold) and Skirmish (play or watch any AI you set up in the AI lab).

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. The game works fully offline and has no accounts or sign-in. Only buying or restoring Full Army needs a connection, because it goes through the App Store.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?

Progress is saved on your iPhone only. It is not synced between devices. An iPhone backup that you restore to a new phone may carry it over. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I start over?

Delete the app and install it again. This erases all progress and settings.

How do I turn off sound, haptics or speed up battles?

Sound has a toggle on the title screen. Haptics is in the pause menu during a match. The speed button during a battle switches between 1×, 2× and 3×.

What is free, and what does Full Army unlock?

The download is free and includes Campaign Acts 1 and 2, the Daily Challenge, Skirmish and the first Breakpoint puzzles, with no time limits. Full Army is a single one-time purchase that opens every campaign act, the Gauntlet and every Breakpoint puzzle for good. There are no ads, subscriptions, coins, energy or loot boxes.

I played before Full Army existed. Do I have to buy it?

No. If you had already reached Act 3 of the campaign, you keep every act you could play, no purchase needed.

How do I get Full Army back on a new iPhone, or after reinstalling?

Open Shop from the title screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Your Full Army unlock comes back. Game progress is separate and stays on the old device (see above).

My purchase is pending, failed or I was charged and nothing unlocked

A pending purchase (for example one waiting for Ask to Buy approval) unlocks by itself once Apple approves it. Keep the app open on the Shop screen, or reopen it later. If it still has not unlocked, tap Restore Purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If none of that helps, email us with the date of the purchase.

What do I need to play?

An iPhone running iOS 15 or later.
