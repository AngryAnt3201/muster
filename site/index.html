MUSTER

build · place · clash

A turn-based army battler for iPhone. Buy units, place them on your half of the field, press Fight and watch your army play out your plan.

Coming soon to the App Store

24 units

No grid, no lanes. Every unit steers freely and fights its own way: flankers, chargers, casters, traps, flyers, builders.

Evolve and Ascend

Rank units from Recruit to Elite, then Ascend them for a new look and a signature ability.

Rivals that think

Rivals test their plans against your board. Tune them yourself in the AI lab, or watch two AIs fight.

Four modes

A 30-level campaign with bosses, the Gauntlet, Breakpoint puzzles and open Skirmish.

Offline

No account, no sign-in, no internet needed. Your progress stays on your device.

Fair

No ads, no in-app purchases, no timers.
