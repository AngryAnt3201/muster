Privacy Policy

Muster: Tactics for iPhone · Effective 6 November 2026 (updated for version 1.1)

Short version: Muster: Tactics collects no data about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The game itself never connects to the internet: the one exception is the optional Full Army purchase, which Apple handles through the App Store. Your game progress is saved only on your own device.

Who we are

Muster: Tactics (“the app”) is made by Jack Wakem, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at jackwakem@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, receive, store on a server, share or sell any personal information or usage data. Specifically, the app:

  • has no sign-up, login or account of any kind;
  • does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or any other permission;
  • contains no analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting software, from us or from anyone else;
  • does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and does not use the advertising identifier;
  • makes no network connections of its own. It works fully offline. Buying or restoring Full Army opens Apple’s App Store purchase sheet, which Apple runs.

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves a small amount of game data in its own storage on your iPhone: campaign stars, Breakpoint puzzle results, your best Gauntlet run, your Daily Challenge streak and best result, whether Full Army is unlocked, the units you have seen, your AI lab settings, and your sound, haptics and battle-speed settings. This data never leaves your device through the app and we cannot see it.

Deleting the app deletes this data. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, the backup may include it; backups are handled by Apple under your own Apple Account and are not accessible to us.

Purchases and payments

The app is free to download and offers one optional in-app purchase, Full Army, a one-time unlock. Apple processes the payment through the App Store under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. We never see your name, Apple Account, card or other payment details. The app only asks Apple whether this device’s Apple Account owns Full Army, and remembers the answer on your iPhone so the unlock works offline. Apple may give us the same anonymous, aggregated sales reports it gives every developer; they do not identify you.

Apple

Downloading the app is handled by Apple through the App Store under Apple’s own privacy policy. If you have chosen in your iPhone’s settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated statistics and crash reports about the app. These come from Apple, do not identify you, and are used only to fix problems.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live).

This website

This site is a few static pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers. The service that hosts it may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and reliability; we do not use them to identify anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. You control the data on your device and can remove it at any time by deleting the app. If you have a question about your privacy, email us and we will reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example because a future version adds a feature that needs data, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released, and the App Store privacy label will change to match.
