Questions

How do I play?

Each round, drag unit cards from the shop at the bottom onto your half of the field. Drag a placed unit to move it, or tap it to rank it up or sell it. When you are ready, tap Fight and the battle plays out on its own. Units come back every round, so your army grows. The loser of each round takes damage; the first commander to reach zero loses the match.

What are ranks and Ascended units?

A placed unit can be ranked up from Recruit to Veteran to Elite, getting tougher and stronger each time. From round 5 you can Ascend it to rank 4, which unlocks that unit’s signature ability.

Which modes are there?

Campaign (60 levels in ten acts, with a boss at the end of each act), the Daily Challenge (one board a day, the same for every player, with a streak to keep), Gauntlet (rival after rival on one health pool, with boons between fights), Breakpoint (one-battle puzzles with fixed gold) and Skirmish (play or watch any AI you set up in the AI lab).

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. The game works fully offline and has no accounts or sign-in. Only an in-app purchase needs a connection, because it goes through the App Store.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?

Progress is saved on your iPhone only. It is not synced between devices. An iPhone backup that you restore to a new phone may carry it over. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I start over?

Delete the app and install it again. This erases all progress and settings.

How do I turn off sound, haptics or speed up battles?

Sound has a toggle on the title screen. Haptics is in the pause menu during a match. The speed button during a battle switches between 1×, 2× and 3×.

What is free?

Everything. Every campaign act, the Gauntlet, every Breakpoint puzzle, the Daily Challenge and Skirmish are free to play, with no time limits. Nothing is locked behind a purchase. Optional purchases only speed you up. There are no ads, subscriptions or energy, and every chest shows its odds.

What is the Starter Pack?

A one-time offer in the Shop: a fixed pack of gems, coins and unit cards to get your army going. You can buy it once; after that it leaves the Shop. It unlocks nothing that isn’t already free.

What are gems?

Gems are the in-game currency. You earn them free from three-star clears, bosses, Daily Challenge wins and streaks, and you can buy packs in the Shop. They buy coins for troop upgrades and a Gauntlet Rally. Every pack gives the exact number of gems shown. Gems, coins and cards are used up when spent and stay on the device, like your game progress. If the app closes during a purchase, it is added the next time you open it.

What is the War Pass?

Each 30-day season has a War Pass of 30 tiers. Three daily quests and the stars or crowns you win fill it, and its free track pays every player coins, cards, chests, gems and a keep skin. War Pass Premium is an optional purchase, once per season, that adds a second track of bigger rewards and makes chests unlock 20% faster. It counts back: rewards for tiers you already reached are ready at once. It never changes a battle.

What are special offers?

Two or three limited-time bundles in the Shop that change every two days. Each shows exactly what it holds (gems, coins, cards for a named unit and one chest) and can be bought once while it is up. The chest's contents are random; tap Odds on the offer to see them before you buy.

My purchase is pending, failed or I was charged and nothing arrived

A pending purchase (for example one waiting for Ask to Buy approval) arrives by itself once Apple approves it. Keep the app open on the Shop screen, or reopen it later: anything paid for but not yet added is added when the app opens. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If none of that helps, email us with the date of the purchase.

What do I need to play?

An iPhone running iOS 15 or later.
