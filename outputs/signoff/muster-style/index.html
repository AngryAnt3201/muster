Muster v3: pick one of three style directions

All three are rendered in Blender from the same unit models, the same 3/4 camera and the same compositing the game will use. Only the direction file changes: outline width and ink, cel bands and shadow tint, rim light, material palette, ground, props, keep, VFX and UI colours. Each card shows six units (Ranger tiers 1 to 4, Stormcaller Ascended, Stone Colossus tier 2), a slice of a battlefield (enemy keep down to past the midline) and a live turntable of the Ranger in all 8 facings.

  • Collision + zones draws each obstacle's collision circle, every unit's body circle, the keep footprint, the board edge and both deploy-zone lines straight from game coordinates through the camera maths in art/style/contract.json. Props sitting on their circles is the check that touch placement still maps exactly.
  • Units are billboards rendered at 35 degrees and drawn 1.3x true size so faces and weapons read on a phone, the ground, props and keep at 55 degrees. Everything is sorted back to front by depth.
  • The turntable renders 5 facings and mirrors SW, W and NW, the same as the atlases will. Only idle and attack keys are shown here, walk and death come with mass production.
  • Everything is original: built from our own procedural models, no traced or reference art.
